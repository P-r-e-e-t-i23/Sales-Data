# Sales target & Regional Performance Dashboard
Here is the structured documentation tailored specifically for your Excel Sales Performance & Target Analysis Dashboard:
1. Project Title / Headline
📊 Excel Sales Executive Target & Regional Performance Dashboard
An interactive, dynamic Microsoft Excel dashboard designed to evaluate sales executive performance, total sales volume, target hit percentages, and variance across multiple regional territories.
2. Short Description / Purpose
The Sales Executive Target Performance Dashboard provides sales leaders and regional managers with a clear, data-driven view of individual sales representative achievements. Built inside Microsoft Excel using Pivot Tables and dynamic formulas, this tool enables real-time tracking of sales volume, target completion percentages, and shortfall metrics across key regional hubs.
3. Tech Stack
• 📊 Microsoft Excel – Core spreadsheet platform used for data storage, calculations, pivot analysis, and reporting.
• ⚙️ Excel Pivot Tables & Pivot Charts – Dynamically aggregates and visualizes sales performance across executives and territories.
• 🔀 Excel Slicers & Dynamic Controls – Interactive top-row city slicers (Chennai, Delhi, Mumbai, Nagpur, Patna, Pune, Ranchi, Surat) for instant interactive filtering.
• 📐 Excel Formulas & Functions – Utilized for calculating target variances, percentages, and summaries across sheets (DASHBOARD, Sheet1, RAW DATA).
• 📁 File Format – .xlsx / .xlsm for workbook distribution and interactive data exploration.
4. Data Source
Source: Sales Target & Operational Records (RAW DATA worksheet).
The underlying dataset tracks individual Sales Executives (e.g., Anikuttan, Arvind Sharma, Jagdish Chandra, Miny Mole, Rachita Anupam, Dinesh Kumar, Omprakash O, Pankaj Shelkey, Prabha Desikan, Praveen Kumar), regional city assignments, total units/revenue sold, individual sales targets, and calculated percentage achievements.
5. Features / Highlights
• Business Problem
Sales directors often struggle to quickly identify top-performing executives, spot representatives missing their targets, and analyze performance differences across various regional branches. Without a consolidated dashboard, evaluating sales targets requires sorting through large, raw spreadsheet tables.
Key business questions addressed:
Which sales executives are generating the highest total sales volume in specific regions?
What percentage of target goals has each sales executive achieved?
How far off are representatives from reaching 100% of their target targets?
How does sales performance vary across major Indian cities (e.g., Nagpur, Delhi, Mumbai)?
• Goal of the Dashboard
To deliver a clean, intuitive Excel report that:
Summarizes sales representative metrics across four distinct performance views (Dashboard 1 through Dashboard 4).
Facilitates seamless cross-filtering by city to analyze regional team outputs.
Highlights individual achievements and target shortfalls to guide sales incentives and management intervention.
• Walkthrough of Key Visuals
Top Region Slicer Bar: Enables single-click interactive filtering across 8 major cities (Chennai, Delhi, Mumbai, Nagpur, Patna, Pune, Ranchi, Surat).
Total Sales by Executive (Table & Horizontal Bar Chart - Dashboard 1): Ranks sales executives (e.g., Jagdish Chandra, Miny Mole, Anikuttan) by raw sales volume to quickly spot top performers.
Secondary Sales Breakdown (Table - Dashboard 2): Highlights total sales figures for secondary regional reps (e.g., Dinesh Kumar, Omprakash O).
Target Hit % Share (Table & Donut/Pie Chart - Dashboard 3): Displays target hit percentages (e.g., ~75%–77%) and visualizes target contribution ratios across the sales team.
Variance / "Away From Target %" (Table & Line Chart - Dashboard 4): Tracks how far each representative is from their target (e.g., ~65%–71%), plotted via a line chart to easily identify reps needing performance support.
• Business Impact & Insights
Performance Management: Helps sales leadership reward top achievers while identifying underperforming representatives who require additional coaching.
Regional Resource Allocation: Comparing city performance allows managers to reallocate sales targets or marketing support to lagging territories.
Goal Alignment: Clear visibility into "Away From Target %" motivates sales teams to close gaps before quarterly deadlines.
